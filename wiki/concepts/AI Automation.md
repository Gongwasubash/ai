# AI Automation

Setting up AI to perform repetitive tasks automatically without manual intervention. Three-part framework:

## The 3 Parts

1. **Trigger** — what starts the automation
   - Time-based (daily at 8am, every Monday)
   - Event-based (button press, WhatsApp message, email, phone call)
   - Manual vs automatic

2. **AI Processing** — the actual work the AI does
   - Fetch data from a source
   - Analyze, summarize, or generate
   - Transform from one format to another

3. **Action** — where the output goes
   - Email, file, message, database, another tool

## Example

"Every day at 8am (trigger), go to weather.com, get my area's weather (processing), and email it to me (action)."

## AI Agent vs Automation

- **Automation** = set of fixed rules, does one thing repeatedly
- **AI Agent** = thinks and decides autonomously, can handle different situations
