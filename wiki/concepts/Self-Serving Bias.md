# Self-Serving Bias

A cognitive bias where the human brain credits positive outcomes to its own character, abilities, and effort, but attributes negative outcomes to external factors or other people's mistakes.

## Impact on Self-Improvement

This bias makes it impossible for the brain to diagnose its own recurring patterns. The same brain creating the problems is the one trying to solve them — so blind spots persist indefinitely.

## How AI Helps

An AI system like the [[Hidden Variable Engine]] can bypass this bias by analyzing your data objectively — finding patterns, contradictions, and mistakes that the brain filters out.
