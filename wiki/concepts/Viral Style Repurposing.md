# Viral Style Repurposing

Keep a proven video's visual DNA, swap its content: re-brief the shot breakdown around your own narration (~52 words per 30s) and regenerate — same style, new message.

## When to Use

- A format is proven viral but its topic isn't yours
- You need high-production look with zero After Effects skill
- Series content where visual consistency matters more than novelty
