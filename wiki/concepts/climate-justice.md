# Climate Justice

The argument that negligible emitters suffering outsized climate damage are owed support — funding, infrastructure, early-warning systems, trained manpower — by major emitting nations.

## The Nepal Case (per WSO)

- Nepal's carbon contribution: negligible; climate damage: Himalayan collapses, flash floods (Melamchi, Rosi, Thame, Seti, Bhote Koshi), India/Pakistan regional parallels
- HKH glaciers −12% area / −9% ice (1990–2020); post-2020 melt rate ~doubled; UNDP: up to 2% of GDP at risk by 2050 without mitigation
- "Khaeko bis lagchha, nakhaeko lagdaina" (only the eater is poisoned) no longer holds — the pollution of rich nations is drunk by Nepal

## Strategy Notes

- Evidence first: attribute events (temperature-driven, not rainfall-driven) before claiming
- Coalition: act jointly with similarly affected nations for long-term leverage
- Parallel track: build domestic resilience now — don't wait for compensation
