# Prompt Engineering

The practice of designing inputs to AI models to get optimal outputs. AI is a pattern matching machine — better prompts = better pattern matches = better responses.

## Core Framework

1. **Role** — tell the AI who to be ("You are a senior data analyst")
2. **Task** — clearly define what to do
3. **Context** — background information it needs
4. **Constraints** — limits (word count, format, tone, what to include/exclude)
5. **Output Format** — exactly what form you want (table, JSON, email, list)

## Techniques

- **One-shot prompting** — provide one example of desired output
- **Few-shot prompting** — provide multiple examples for better style/tone matching
- **Chain of Thought** — append "Let's think step by step" to break complex problems into smaller parts

## Why It Matters

99% of people use AI like a search engine. Prompt engineering is what separates basic users from power users. The same model gives vastly different results based on how you prompt it.
