# Glacial Collapse Flood

A flood triggered not by rain but by falling glacier ice + bedrock — which melts through friction into a mud-debris surge that grows as it entrains sediment downstream.

## Mechanism (Langtang Lirung case)

1. Warm years (+0.58°C) accelerate melt; water pools in glacier cracks and rock joints
2. Thawing permafrost loosens joints holding cliffs together
3. Ice + bedrock slab detaches (~10 football fields), falls ~1,200m
4. Impact/friction liquefies ice into mud slurry; narrow steep gorge amplifies it
5. Surge hits first settlement ~7 minutes later

## Why It Matters for Nepal

- Unpredictable timing: which lake/slope fails, and when, can't be forecast exactly
- Prior events: Melamchi, Rosi, Thame, Seti flash floods — same family
- 47 risky glacial lakes in Koshi/Gandaki/Karnali basins; 6 high-risk
