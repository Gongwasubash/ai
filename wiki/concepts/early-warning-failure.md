# Early Warning Failure

Having a forecast system is not the same as getting the right message to the right people in time — the Bhote Koshi case shows every link in the chain can hold while the outcome still fails.

## Failure Chain (Bhadra 10)

1. **Detection lag:** gauge data stopped 8:50; forecasting branch learned of the flood only via NDRRMA's 9 AM verification request
2. **Dissemination lag:** SMS/public alerts ~15 min after confirmation — after Timure was already gone
3. **Scale gap:** Betrawati was told "flood coming" but not "catastrophic" — no effective evacuation
4. **Coordination gap:** weak local-government liaison, missing equipment/manpower, contradictory media reports (Trishuli 3B)

## Lesson

Warnings must carry *scale + action* ("leave now, go high"), not just existence — plus redundant channels, local drills, and honest post-event audits.
