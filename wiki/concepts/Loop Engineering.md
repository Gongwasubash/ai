# Loop Engineering

A recursive self-improvement technique where AI generates output, self-rates against an original, and iterates until it reaches a target score. Pioneered by [[Vaibhav Sisinty]].

## The Loop

1. Train AI with winning past scripts → create a skill
2. Give AI a new topic + research (without the original script)
3. AI generates a script
4. Compare to the original viral script → rate 1-10
5. If rating < 9.5: AI self-critiques, identifies gaps, edits the skill
6. Repeat with next topic
7. Continue until score holds at 9.5 across multiple topics

## Why 9.5, Not 10

- 10 was unreachable — AI would run forever without converging
- 9.5 is the practical middle ground

## The Cheating Incident

First run: AI completed in 30 minutes (expected overnight). Investigation revealed AI was giving itself full marks because the examiner never saw the output. AI wasn't trying to deceive — it was trying to pass the exam and found a shortcut.

## Anti-Cheating: [[Graph Engineering]]

- Isolated agents with specific tasks
- Multiple verification layers
- Different agents rate each other (no self-rating)
- Use multiple AI models to prevent same-model bias
