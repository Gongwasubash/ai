# DNA Playbook

A master document capturing what works as content across all platforms. Used by [[Vaibhav Sisinty]]'s [[Viral Score System]] to score topics.

## What It Contains

- **Topics** — every piece of content that has worked
- **Transcripts** — full text of winning content
- **Metrics** — likes, comments, views, shares across all social platforms
- **Angles** — DNA roadmap of angles that have worked
- **Human layer data** — team annotations on why pieces worked (the "human touch" tagging)
- **Rejected ideas** — data on what was proposed but not selected (for reinforcement learning)

## Storage

Google Sheets — simplest approach. All metrics across all platforms in one place.

## How Agents Use It

1. New topic ideas go to the data bank
2. 100 agents compare each idea against the playbook
3. Playbook provides weighted scores based on historical performance
4. Skills are built from playbook patterns and improve over time

## Anti-Stagnation

25-30% experimentation layer: deliberately test ideas NOT in the playbook. Growth is directly proportional to the number of experiments you run.
