# Hidden Variable Engine

An AI system that analyzes your second brain data to uncover the single biggest blind spot or behavioral pattern holding you back. Built by [[Praful Sharma]].

## Problem It Solves

The human brain has a **self-serving bias** — it can't diagnose itself because the same brain creating the problems is the one trying to solve them. We ignore our own excuses, recurring mistakes, contradictions, and blind spots.

## Architecture — 5 AI Agents

1. **Pattern Detection Agent** — scans journal entries, content consumed, and actions to find repeated behaviors, complaints, desires, frustrations, excuses, and recurrent themes
2. **Contradiction Detection Agent** — compares thoughts/speech/actions against goals to find mismatches (e.g., wanting YouTube success but only doing research, wanting fitness but eating poorly)
3. **Behavior Analysis Agent** — determines operational patterns: do you finish projects? do you research but never execute? do you keep switching projects?
4. **Blind Spot Discovery Agent** — scores each mistake by how unaware you are of it
5. **Hidden Variable Identification Agent** — selects the single most impactful root cause from all agent reports

## Data Sources (from Second Brain)

- Journal entries (day-to-day activities)
- Goals
- Past decisions
- Ideas
- Failures and mistakes
- CRM data
- Notes and resources

## Output

A report containing:
- The hidden variable (the one thing to fix first)
- Why it matters
- Real-life impact
- Evidence locations (journal entries, patterns, decisions, contradictions, screenshots)
- What it costs you

## Cycle

Monthly: work for a month, update the second brain, re-run the engine, get a new report.
