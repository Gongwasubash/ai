# Use Fish Audio to Direct Every Line of Your AI Voice

**Source:** YouTube Short by [[Jack Roberts]] — [Watch](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/8Q5_zWTsB2Q)

## Summary

Fish Audio is a voice cloning + TTS platform where you direct every line with open-ended bracket tags. Clone a voice from 10 seconds, paste your script, add delivery tags like `[whisper]`, `[laugh]`, `[serious]` per line, generate MP3, then lip-sync with HeyGen Character 3. Fish 2.1 Pro supports 83 languages.

## Key Takeaways

- **5-step workflow:** Open Fish Audio → clone voice (10s clip) → paste script with bracket tags → generate MP3 → lip-sync in HeyGen Character 3
- **Bracket directing:** `[whisper]`, `[laugh]`, `[loud]`, `[serious]` — per-line delivery tags
- **Open-ended tags:** Fish 2.1 Pro accepts any delivery tag, not just predefined ones
- **83 languages:** Multilingual voice cloning and TTS
- **Community voices:** Grab pre-made voices or clone your own
