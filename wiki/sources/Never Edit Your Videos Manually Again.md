# Never Edit Your Videos Manually Again

**Source:** YouTube Short by [[Jack Roberts]] — [Watch](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/8oZP9PsRKZ8)

## Summary

Chat Cut is the first AI video editor that lives inside Claude Code. Edit videos with natural language prompts — cut dead air, tighten intros, add b-roll — and get a fully editable timeline. Can also create motion graphics and generate AI b-roll footage. Save your editing workflow as a reusable skill for future videos.

## Key Takeaways

- **Edit inside Claude Code:** First AI video editor that lives in a coding agent
- **Natural language prompts:** "Cut the dead air. Tighten up the intro. Pull that b-roll under this line."
- **Editable timeline:** Unlike pure-AI editors, you can still manually move things around
- **Motion graphics + AI b-roll:** Creates engaging footage, not just cuts
- **Save as skill:** Workflow saved as a reusable skill — agent learns your editing preferences for future videos
