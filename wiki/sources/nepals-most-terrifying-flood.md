# Nepal's Most Terrifying Flood — WSO

**Source:** YouTube video by [[Why So Offended ?]] (written by Deepak Paudel & [[Binayak Kuikel]])
**Date:** 2026-09-04
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zwtEBBYZ2Xs

## Summary

WSO reconstructs the catastrophic Bhadra 10 flash flood on the [[Bhote Koshi Flood 2026|Bhote Koshi]] in Rasuwa — from a [[Glacial Collapse Flood|glacial collapse]] at Langtang Lirung (~5,200m) to the destruction of Timure market in seconds — then audits the state's [[Early Warning Failure|early-warning and rescue response]], and closes with the [[Climate Justice]] case: Nepal emits almost nothing yet pays the price.

## Key Takeaways

### The Event
- Wednesday morning ~8:45 AM: Timure market normal; minutes later a mud-laden surge erased it — "bustling Timure became ruins in seconds"
- ~2,000+ people in the Timure–Rasuwaagadhi zone (customs agents, laborers, hotel staff, road workers, Mansarovar pilgrims); 40–50 concrete buildings (4–5 stories) fully destroyed; customs office, quarantine, police post gone
- Flood route: Rasuwaagadhi (entry point, ~7 min from slide) → Ghattekhola → Timure → Chilime hydropower belt → Syafrubesi (~9 AM) → Hakubesi/Trishuli projects → Mailung → Upper Trishuli 3A/3B → Betrawati (~40–42 km) → onward to Devghat by ~3:20 PM

### The Cause
- Glacier ice + bedrock collapse (~0.72 km², ~10 football fields) fell ~1,200m; energy registered as a 4.4-magnitude seismic event at 8:37 AM (NYT video corroboration)
- Chinese report: no heavy rainfall trigger — 2024–26 satellite data showed cracks from zero movement at the snout; spring/summer 2026 ran ~0.58°C hotter, accelerating melt into cracks; thawing permafrost destabilized rock joints
- Timure's geography amplified it: tiny steep valley, no floodplain for the river to spread

### The Response Gap
- Flood Forecasting Division had no official word until NDRRMA's 9 AM verification request; Syafrubesi gauge data had stopped at 8:50; SMS alerts went ~15 min after confirmation
- Betrawati got advance word but not the scale — evacuation ineffective, heavy losses (~200 households)
- Rescue claimed: ~12,000 rescued, 21,314 security personnel, 610 helicopter flights, NPR 16 crore relief (Rasuwa/Nuwakot/Dhading + 15 local units)
- Contradictory media reports (esp. Trishuli 3B tunnel: experts deployed vs. nobody inside vs. 201 missing); Upper Trishuli 3A tunnel rescues (2 alive Friday); weak local coordination, missing equipment, slow international help

### The Lesson
- Bhadra-10 was unexpected but not unimaginable — a 2015 post-quake study already flagged Rasuwa (rugged topography, complex geology, improper land use)
- 47 risky glacial lakes in Koshi/Gandaki/Karnali basins (25 Tibet, 21 Nepal, 1 India); 6 high-risk (Chorolpa, Lower Barun, Imja, Lumding West, Chamjang, Thulagi); HKH glaciers −12% area / −9% ice 1990–2020, melt rate ~doubled after 2020
- "Khaeko bis lagchha, nakhaeko lagdaina" no longer holds — Nepal must push [[Climate Justice]] while building warning systems, infrastructure, and capable manpower now
