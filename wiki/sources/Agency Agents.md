# Agency Agents — 280 AI Employees for Your Business

**Source:** YouTube Short by [[Jack Roberts]] — [Watch](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/2iKfjKjAhrw)

## Summary

Agency Agents is a free repo containing 280 specialist AI employees — engineers, designers, marketers, sales people, even lawyers. Each is a proper specialist with its own tools and capabilities. One install drops all 280 into your laptop. Stops the "one assistant wearing multiple hats" problem.

## Key Takeaways

- **280 specialist agents:** Engineers, designers, marketers, sales, lawyers — each a proper specialist
- **Not one multitasking assistant:** Each agent has its own tools and capabilities
- **Free and local:** One install drops all 280 onto your laptop
- **Specialist-first architecture:** Stop asking one AI to be everything; each AI does one thing well
