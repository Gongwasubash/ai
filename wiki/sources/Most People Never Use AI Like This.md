# Most People Never Use AI Like This (My Complete Workflow)

YouTube tutorial by [[Praful Sharma]] on building a **Hidden Variable Engine** — an AI system that analyzes second brain data to uncover hidden patterns, blind spots, contradictions, and behaviors limiting progress.

## Key Takeaways

- The human brain has a **self-serving bias**: it credits successes to itself and blames failures on external factors. This makes it blind to its own recurring mistakes.
- A second brain holds: journal entries, goals, past decisions, ideas, failures/mistakes, CRM data, notes/resources
- The Hidden Variable Engine uses **5 AI agents** working together:
  1. **Pattern Detection Agent** — finds repeated behaviors, complaints, desires, frustrations, excuses
  2. **Contradiction Detection Agent** — identifies gaps between what you think/say/do vs. your goals
  3. **Behavior Analysis Agent** — determines how you actually operate (start vs. finish projects, research vs. execute)
  4. **Blind Spot Discovery Agent** — scores each mistake by how unaware you are of it
  5. **Hidden Variable Identification Agent** — picks the single biggest root cause of your problems
- The final report includes: the hidden variable, why it matters, impact on life, where AI found evidence, and what it costs you
- Runs on a **monthly cycle** — work for a month, update the second brain, then re-run the analysis
- All data stays **local** on the machine — AI only gets access you explicitly grant

## Contrast with Basic Second Brain

This extends the basic [[Second Brain]] pattern (Karpathy's LLM Wiki) with an **analytical layer**. The second brain collects and organizes; the Hidden Variable Engine actively interrogates the data to find what you can't see about yourself.
