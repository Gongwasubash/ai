# Google's 8 Hour AI Essentials Course In 18 Minutes

Comprehensive AI fundamentals tutorial by [[Praful Sharma]], condensing Google's 8-hour course into 18 minutes. Covers AI basics through advanced usage.

## Key Takeaways

### AI Basics
- **AI** = machine/software that performs tasks requiring human intelligence
- **Machine Learning** = AI that learns patterns from data instead of being programmed with rules
- **Deep Learning** = ML with neural networks that mimic human brain structure
- **3 types of AI**: Narrow (single task), General (LLMs, image gen), Super Intelligence (doesn't exist yet)

### How LLMs Work
- AI doesn't understand words — it understands **tokens** (word fragments stored as numbers in vector databases)
- **Context window** = AI's memory whiteboard. Fills up, older info gets dropped (why AI "forgets" mid-conversation)
- LLMs are **prediction machines** — trained to predict the next token
- Knowledge vs Reasoning: AI is great at facts (knowledge), struggles with novel logic problems (reasoning)

### Prompt Engineering Framework
A good prompt needs:
1. **Role** — who should the AI be?
2. **Task** — what should it do?
3. **Context** — background info it needs
4. **Constraints** — limits (word count, format, style)
5. **Output Format** — PDF, table, JSON, email, etc.

### Prompting Techniques
- **One-shot** — give one example of what you want
- **Few-shot** — give multiple examples for better matching
- **Chain of Thought** — "Let's think step by step" to break complex problems into parts

### AI Automation (3 parts)
1. **Trigger** — how it starts (time, button, message, email)
2. **AI Processing** — the actual work (fetch data, analyze, generate)
3. **Action** — where output goes (email, file, message)

### Top 1% AI User Mindset
- Don't use AI as a search engine (Google already exists)
- Understand AI is a prediction machine → use it to its advantage
- Master prompting → master tools → build automations
- Tools mentioned: ChatGPT, Gemini, NotebookLM, Claude, Codex, n8n, vAPI
