# Refero Styles — 2,000 Free Design Files

**Source:** YouTube Short by [[Jack Roberts]] — [Watch](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/STyH-htETPY)

## Summary

Refero Styles is a library of 2,000+ design styles as individual MD files, each describing how a product looks — written for AI to read and replicate. Pick styles from the explore page, combine multiple with your own ideas, and apply them to your site in ~10 seconds.

## Key Takeaways

- **2,000+ design styles:** Each is a single MD file describing a product's visual design
- **AI-readable format:** Written specifically for AI to read and replicate
- **Combine and customize:** Mix multiple styles with your own ideas for unique results
- **10-second application:** Pick a style from explore page, apply to your website
