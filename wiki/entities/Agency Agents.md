# Agency Agents

Free repo containing 280 specialist AI employees — engineers, designers, marketers, sales people, even lawyers. Each is a proper specialist with its own tools and capabilities. One install drops all 280 onto your laptop.

## Architecture

- 280 specialist agents (not one multitasking assistant)
- Each agent has its own tools and capabilities
- Categories: engineers, designers, marketers, sales, lawyers
- Free and local (runs on your laptop)
