# Google Flow

Google's AI filmmaking tool (labs.google/fx/tools/flow): project-based video generation with persistent characters (face + voice), @-taggable scene/subject/voice assets, and the Omni video models.

## Known Details

- **URL:** https://labs.google/fx/tools/flow
- **Omni 1.1 Flash:** 10-second cap per clip; needs sound-design prompt line + no-VO negative prompt; occasionally bakes in VO anyway
- **Characters:** upload image, name it, attach preset or description-generated voice, optional personality line
- **Limits that shape workflow:** 32 words dialogue per 10s clip; generate one clip at a time to save credits
