# n8n

Open-source workflow automation platform. Self-hostable alternative to Zapier.

## What It Is

Visual workflow builder that connects APIs, services, and AI models. Runs on your own server (data stays local).

## Key Features

- **Visual editor** — drag-and-drop workflow building
- **300+ integrations** — Google Docs, Sheets, SMTP, OpenAI, Apify, etc.
- **Self-hosted** — your data stays on your server
- **AI nodes** — built-in OpenAI, Claude, etc. integration
- **Cron jobs** — schedule workflows to run automatically
- **Loop/batch processing** — handle large datasets

## Comparison

| Feature | n8n | Zapier | Make |
|---------|-----|--------|------|
| Self-hosted | Yes | No | No |
| Open source | Yes | No | No |
| AI nodes | Built-in | Limited | Limited |
| Cost | Free (self-hosted) | $20+/mo | $9+/mo |
| Learning curve | Medium | Low | Medium |

## Why It Matters

n8n is the "IDE" for AI automation. Just as [[Obsidian]] is the IDE for knowledge management, n8n is the IDE for building AI workflows. It's where you connect AI models to real-world actions.
