# Bhote Koshi Flood 2026

Catastrophic Bhadra-10 flash flood on the Bhote Koshi river, Rasuwa — triggered by glacial collapse at Langtang Lirung, destroying Timure market in seconds and running ~100+ km to Devghat.

## Facts (per WSO, Sept 2026 — tolls evolving)

- **Trigger:** ~0.72 km² glacier ice + bedrock collapse from ~5,200m, ~1,200m drop; 4.4-magnitude seismic signal at 8:37 AM
- **Worst hit:** Timure market (40–50 concrete buildings, customs/immigration posts erased); ~2,000+ people in impact zone
- **Route:** Rasuwaagadhi → Timure → Chilime → Syafrubesi → Hakubesi → Mailung → Upper Trishuli 3A/3B → Betrawati → Devghat (~3:20 PM)
- **Hydropower hit:** Chilime, Sanjen, Langtang, Trishuli (216 MW), Mailung, Upper Trishuli 3A/3B — staff trapped/missing
- **State response:** ~12,000 rescued, 21,314 personnel, 610 heli flights, NPR 16 crore relief; alert delay ~15+ min past confirmation; Betrawati warned of flood but not of scale
