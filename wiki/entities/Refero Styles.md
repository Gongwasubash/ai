# Refero Styles

Library of 2,000+ design styles as individual MD files, each describing how a product looks — written for AI to read and replicate. Pick styles from the explore page, combine multiple with your own ideas, and apply to your site in ~10 seconds.

## How It Works

- Each style is a single MD file describing a product's visual design
- Written in AI-readable format
- Browse styles on the explore page
- Combine multiple styles with custom ideas
- Apply to website in ~10 seconds
